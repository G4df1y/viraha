# Fitness Training Knowledge

## Progressive Overload
The foundation of all strength and muscle growth. Gradually increase weight, reps, or sets over time.

- **Double Progression**: Hit the upper rep range (e.g., 12) for all sets before increasing weight
- **Linear Progression**: Add 2.5-5kg each session (works for beginners 3-6 months)
- **Double Progression Method**: Stay at same weight until you can do 2 more reps than target on all sets

## Training Splits

### Push/Pull/Legs (PPL)
- Push: chest, shoulders, triceps
- Pull: back, biceps, rear delts
- Legs: quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves
- Frequency: 3-6 days/week
- Best for: intermediate, 4-6 days available

### Upper/Lower
- Upper: chest, back, shoulders, arms
- Lower: quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves
- Frequency: 4 days/week
- Best for: balanced development, 4 days available

### Full Body
- All major muscle groups each session
- Frequency: 3 days/week
- Best for: beginners, 3 days available, frequency-focused

### PHUL (Power Hypertrophy Upper Lower)
- 2 power days (heavy, low reps) + 2 hypertrophy days (moderate, higher reps)
- Frequency: 4 days/week
- Best for: intermediate, strength + size combo

## Training Principles

### RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)
- RPE 10: absolute max, can't do another rep
- RPE 9: one rep left in tank
- RPE 8: two reps left
- RPE 7: three reps left
- For most hypertrophy work: RPE 7-9

### Rest Periods
- Strength (1-5 reps): 3-5 min
- Hypertrophy (6-15 reps): 60-90 sec
- Endurance (15+ reps): 30-60 sec

### Tempo
- Eccentric (lowering): 2-4 seconds
- Concentric (lifting): 1-2 seconds
- Pause at stretch: 0-1 seconds

## Beginner Guidelines
- Start with full body 3x/week
- Focus on form before weight
- 8-12 rep range for most exercises
- 2-3 sets per exercise
- 20-30 working sets per week total
- Progress: add 2.5kg or 2 reps per week

## Common Mistakes
- Ego lifting (too heavy, bad form)
- Not enough protein (target 1.6-2.2g/kg bodyweight)
- Skipping leg day
- No warm-up or cool-down
- Inconsistent schedule
- Comparing to others

## Warm-up Protocol
- 5-10 min light cardio
- Dynamic stretching for target muscles
- 2 warm-up sets at 50% and 75% of working weight
- Movement prep: light version of first exercise
