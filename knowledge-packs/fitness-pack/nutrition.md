# Nutrition Knowledge

## Calorie Basics
- 1kg of body fat ≈ 7700 calories
- Safe fat loss: 0.5-1% body weight per week
- Maintenance calories: bodyweight(kg) × 24-30 (varies by activity)

## Macronutrients

### Protein
- Target: 1.6-2.2g per kg bodyweight
- Essential for muscle repair and growth
- Sources: chicken, fish, eggs, dairy, tofu, whey
- Timing: spread across 3-4 meals
- Absorptive limit per meal: ~40-50g for most people (debated)

### Carbohydrates
- Target: 3-5g per kg (varies by activity)
- Primary energy source for training
- Prioritize complex carbs: oats, rice, potatoes, whole grains
- Simple carbs around workout for quick energy

### Fats
- Target: 0.8-1.2g per kg bodyweight
- Essential for hormone production (testosterone, etc.)
- Sources: olive oil, nuts, avocado, fatty fish
- Minimum 15-20% of calories from fat

## Meal Timing

### Pre-workout (2-3 hours before)
- Balanced meal with carbs + protein
- Low fat to aid digestion
- Example: chicken + rice + vegetables

### Post-workout (within 2 hours)
- Protein + carbs for recovery
- Protein shake + banana is a classic
- Real food is as good as supplements

### Intermittent Fasting
- Can work for fat loss (calorie restriction tool)
- Not magic — still need calorie deficit
- May affect performance for some people
- 16:8 protocol is most common

## Diet Approaches

### Calorie Counting
- Most accurate method
- Use food scale for first 2-3 weeks
- Apps: MyFitnessPal, Cronometer
- Adjust based on weekly weight trend, not daily

### IIFYM (If It Fits Your Macros)
- Flexible dieting — any food works within macros
- 80/20 rule: 80% whole foods, 20% flexible
- Better adherence long-term

### Volume Eating
- Low calorie density foods for fullness
- Vegetables, soups, salads
- Great for managing hunger during deficit

## Common Nutrition Mistakes
- Eating too little (starvation mode is real, slows metabolism)
- Not tracking consistently
- Liquid calories (soda, juice, alcohol)
- Overestimating exercise calorie burn
- Underestimating portion sizes
- No protein with breakfast

## Supplements That Work
- Whey protein: convenient protein source
- Creatine monohydrate: 5g/day, proven strength gains
- Vitamin D: most people deficient
- Caffeine: pre-workout performance boost
- Fish oil: general health

## Hydration
- 2-3L water per day minimum
- More on training days
- Thirst is late indicator — drink consistently
- Electrolytes if sweating heavily
