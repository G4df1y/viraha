# Recovery Knowledge

## Sleep

### Why Sleep Matters
- Growth hormone release primarily during deep sleep
- Muscle repair and protein synthesis peak during sleep
- Cortisol regulation (stress hormone)
- Appetite regulation (ghrelin/leptin balance)
- Performance: sleep-deprived = significantly weaker

### Sleep Guidelines
- 7-9 hours for most people
- Consistent sleep/wake time (even weekends)
- No screens 30-60 min before bed
- Cool room temperature (18-20°C)
- No caffeine after 2pm

### Sleep Hygiene
- Dark room (blackout curtains)
- No phone in bed
- Wind-down routine: reading, stretching, meditation
- Same wake time regardless of sleep duration

## Stress Management

### Cortisol Effects
- High cortisol = muscle breakdown, fat storage (especially belly)
- Impairs recovery and immune function
- Can stall fat loss even with calorie deficit

### Stress Reduction Methods
- Daily walks (10-20 min)
- Deep breathing (4-7-8 method)
- Light stretching/mobility work
- Journaling or meditation
- Reduce unnecessary cardio when stressed

## Muscle Soreness (DOMS)

### What is DOMS
- Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness
- Peaks 24-72 hours after training
- Normal for beginners or new exercises
- NOT an indicator of workout quality

### Managing Soreness
- Light activity (walking, mobility) helps more than complete rest
- Foam rolling for relief
- Heat therapy for blood flow
- Protein intake supports repair
- Sleep is the best recovery tool

## Active Recovery
- Light walking: 10-30 min
- Mobility work: 10-15 min
- Stretching: hold 30 sec per movement
- No heavy lifting — this is recovery, not training
- Active recovery > complete rest for most people

## Deload Weeks
- Take every 4-8 weeks
- Reduce volume by 40-60%
- Keep intensity the same
- Purpose: joint recovery, CNS recovery, prevent burnout
- Signs you need a deload: stalled progress, poor sleep, irritable, excess soreness

## Injury Prevention
- Proper warm-up before every session
- Don't ego lift — form > weight
- Progress load slowly (no more than 5-10% increase per week)
- Listen to sharp pain vs muscle soreness
- Address imbalances early
- Mobility work 2-3x per week

## Recovery Metrics
- Resting heart rate trend
- Sleep quality (not just duration)
- Morning readiness (how do you feel?)
- HRV (Heart Rate Variability) if available
- Grip strength trend
